The School's Namesake

The Legacy of General Manuel Tinio

General Manuel Tinio y Bundoc — revolutionary hero, Governor of Nueva Ecija, and namesake of this school — embodies the courage and civic duty the institution seeks to instill. His memorabilia, letters, and portraits anchor the museum's permanent collection.

This digital archive makes that legacy reachable from any classroom and any phone, pairing physical exhibits with QR-linked narratives.
