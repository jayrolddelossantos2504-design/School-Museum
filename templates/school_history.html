Our Story

History of General Tinio National High School

From a rented private house in 1946 to one of the biggest schools in Nueva Ecija.

The General Tinio National High School of Nueva Ecija is one, if not the biggest, school in the province. Formerly known as Papaya Provincial High School, it was established in 1946 through the efforts of Schools Division Superintendent Mr. Cenon Monasterial Sr., the Parent Teacher Association, Mayor Getulio Bote Sr., and other civic leaders of the town.

Newborn as it was, the Papaya Provincial High School had a population of 90 students. There were only two year levels — first and second year — with one section each. Since the school started from nothing, classes were held in private houses rented by the school. From these humble beginnings, the school grew into a complete high school, was renamed, nationalized, and expanded into the modern campus it is today.

Historical Timeline

Milestones and school heads through the years.

  1. 1946 · Founding

    Papaya Provincial High School

    The school was established with 90 students in the first and second year, one section each, holding classes in rented private houses. In 1946–1947, its first officer-in-charge was Porfirio Yuson.

  2. 1947–1952 · Growth

    Mr. Pedro Magno

    Mr. Pedro Magno was assigned principal. Through the effort of the PTA members and its president Mr. Diosdado Bote, the school acquired 10 hectares of land; the deed of donation was signed by Mayor Gerardo Rivera.

    In 1947–1948, the school became a complete high school with classes from first to fourth year. Classes moved from private houses into a six-room Gabaldon building financed and built through the joint efforts of Ex-Mayor Getulio Bote Sr., the PTA, and civic-minded citizens. The first commencement exercises produced 31 graduates.

  3. 1952–1957 · A New Name

    Ms. Rosario B. Mangulabnan

    Ms. Rosario B. Mangulabnan took over as administrator. During her time, Papaya Provincial High School was changed to General Tinio High School on August 19, 1957.

  4. 1962 · Academic Excellence

    Ranked First in Nueva Ecija

    The school placed first among the twelve public high schools in Nueva Ecija in the 1962 national examination for seniors. It also placed seventh in Filipino IV with a rating of 87.7, credited in part to Ms. Sabina B. Mangulabnan, who was both principal and a Filipino teacher at the time.

  5. 1983–1984 · Nationalization

    Batas Pambansa Blg. 507

    By virtue of Batas Pambansa Blg. 507, signed by former President Ferdinand E. Marcos on June 10, 1983, the school was nationalized, though full operation as a national school began in March 1984. The bill was sponsored by Assemblyman Narcisio S. Nario. Nationalization brought favorable growth in facilities and improvements compared to the earlier years of financial difficulty.

  6. 1991–1998

    Mr. Alfredo I. Bartolome

    Mr. Alfredo I. Bartolome, who hailed from Muñoz, Nueva Ecija, took over the school. Under his leadership, General Tinio National High School showed not only a remarkable face-lift but also outstanding academic performance.

  7. 1999 · Expansion

    Mr. Antonio D. Gante

    On July 1, 1999, Mr. Antonio D. Gante — an alumnus of the institution and a resident of Rio Chico, General Tinio — assumed office. He renewed and re-strengthened the school's mission and vision toward the total development of students and the professional growth of teachers. He spearheaded facility improvements, made students academically competitive, and encouraged teachers to pursue graduate studies and attend seminars and conferences.

    The school gained two spacious halls (the Sotto Hall and the Multi-Purpose Hall) and an adequate library. The thirty-five classrooms before became forty-five with the construction of the Villareal Building and the eight-room JICA building, courtesy of the Japanese government.

  8. 2007 · 21st Century Leadership

    Dr. Nicanor R. Ventura

    On September 13, 2007, Dr. Nicanor R. Ventura, who hailed from Cabiao, Nueva Ecija, became principal. The school acquired 20 computer units, 30 units of speech laboratory with complete onboard table, DVD player, cassette tape recorder, a projector, white screen, and all the needed peripherals. The school was unanimously chosen as CD IV's representative as Best School Implementer of the School-Based Management (SBM) Program of the Department of Education, and it also opened Special Science Class sections.

  9. 2009 · Service to Students

    Dr. Eladio R. Santiago

    On January 26, 2009, Dr. Eladio R. Santiago — a native of Muñoz who resided in Bongabon, Nueva Ecija — became principal. His commitment and dedication earned him recognition as the most outstanding principal in the Division of Nueva Ecija. He introduced the "Adopt a Student Program," believed to be the first of its kind in the country, to answer the burden of miscellaneous fees on parents with support from alumni.

    His efforts brought a new speech laboratory that also serves as an audio-visual room, more computer units donated by alumni and the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI), a six-classroom building, repair of the SUPRA stage, installation of water supply with aid from the LGU, a Science Laboratory, and a functional water system donated by alumni.

  10. 2013–2014 · Facilities

    Buildings and a Gymnasium

    In school year 2013–2014, the school received 50 additional sets of computers from DepEd and acquired 14 new teachers. It was also blessed with a two-classroom building used as a model home for TLE, donated by the Local Government Unit; a two-storey building with 18 classrooms donated through Public-Private Partnership (PPP); and a multi-purpose gymnasium donated by the Provincial Government. The "Dream Come True" multi-purpose gymnasium, located parallel to the PPP building, materialized in 2013.

  11. 2014 · Total Make-Over

    Mrs. Josephine S. Martinez

    In May 2014, Mrs. Josephine S. Martinez, a native of Barangay Bago, General Tinio, and fresh from San Mariano National High School, became School Principal IV. The school underwent a "total make-over": idle lands were converted into different areas of assignment; a gymnasium (covered court) was donated by 1-SAGIP Party List Representative Erlinda M. Santiago; and a cemented pathway from the eastern gate to the school gym was built through the effort of Mayor Virgilio A. Bote and the General Tinio LGU.

    In July 2015, a steel gate was constructed on the eastern side, replacing the old and dilapidated gate. Beside the new senior high school gym rose two consecutive two-storey six-classroom buildings donated by the Provincial Government under Aurelio M. Umali. The Non-Teaching Center, Park, and Guard House were built between the Sotto Hall and the PPP Building, and on April 18, 2016 the Walking Shed "Adopt-a-Post" Project was started with a donation from Batch '86.

  12. 2016 · Change Has Come

    Dr. Cecilio M. Tulaban

    On June 10, 2016, Dr. Cecilio M. Tulaban was installed as School Principal IV. A native of Mambangnan, San Leonardo, Nueva Ecija, he is described as simple, considerate, strict, and firm in his decisions, and supportive of school events involving students. Within his first year he initiated the construction of the school quadrangle, which became a venue for school activities and the Papaya Festival Centennial Celebration Street Dance Competition, with the support of Mayor Engr. Ferdinand P. Bote. He also put up fences around the school quadrangle and initiated the construction of school fences to secure the perimeter and premises.

  13. 2018 · Strategic Direction

    Mrs. Miriam S. Monteroso

    On September 2, 2018, Mrs. Miriam S. Monteroso assumed the principalship. A diligent school head, she provided strategic direction toward quality education. Within ten months, GTNHS emerged as champion in Brigada Eskwela for the Mega School Category in Congressional District IV and third runner-up in the Division. A three-time Natatanging Lingkod Awardee — the highest award in DepEd Nueva Ecija — she accomplished much in physical and academic aspects during her two-year stay.

  14. 2020 · A Premier School

    Mrs. Adora B. Dela Cruz

    On July 6, 2020, Mrs. Adora B. Dela Cruz, another Natatanging Lingkod Hall of Famer, assumed the headship of GTNHS. Under her leadership, the school became an awardee in DepEd Matatag School Implementer, a prestigious honor achieved through a strong partnership with Brigada Eskwela from 2021 to 2024. The school also emerged as champion in the Division Learning Expo 2025, showcasing its commitment to innovative teaching and learning strategies.

    She created a strong mechanism of supervision, monitoring, and evaluation, and the school facilities gained improvements: the principal's office and school parks were enhanced, comfort rooms were rehabilitated, the Junior and Senior High School gyms were renovated, and the Sotto Hall was fully rehabilitated and air-conditioned. With these, the school continues to look forward to greater things in the years to come.

Continuing Legacy

From 90 students and rented private houses in 1946, General Tinio National High School has grown into one of the biggest schools in Nueva Ecija. Its story is one of community effort, academic excellence, and steady progress — a legacy the Heritage Museum and Digital Archive exists to preserve.
