Visit the Museum

Location

Find the Heritage Museum on the main campus of General Tinio National High School.

{{ icons.icon('pin', 'w-5 h-5') }} Address

Heritage Hall, Main Campus

General Tinio National High School, General Tinio, Nueva Ecija, Philippines. The gallery is on the ground floor, beside the main administration building.

{{ icons.icon('clock', 'w-5 h-5') }} Visiting Hours

Monday to Friday

08:00 – 16:00. Last entry at 15:30. The museum follows the official school calendar and is closed on weekends and public holidays.

{{ icons.icon('ticket', 'w-5 h-5') }} Admission

Free for Everyone

Learners, staff, alumni, and guests are welcome at no cost. Group visits and guided tours can be arranged in advance.

Getting Here

Enter through the main gate of General Tinio National High School and follow the museum signs toward Heritage Hall. Visitors arriving by private vehicle may use the designated drop-off area near the administration building.

For guided group visits, please coordinate with the Heritage Committee ahead of your arrival so a docent can meet your group at the entrance.
