Visit the Museum

Location

Find the Heritage Museum on the main campus of General Tinio National High School.

{{ icons.icon('pin', 'w-5 h-5') }} Address

Heritage Hall, Main Campus

General Tinio National High School, General Tinio, Nueva Ecija, Philippines. The gallery is on the ground floor, beside the main administration building.

{{ icons.icon('clock', 'w-5 h-5') }} Visiting Hours

Monday to Friday

08:00 – 16:00. Last entry at 15:30. The museum follows the official school calendar and is closed on weekends and public holidays.

{{ icons.icon('ticket', 'w-5 h-5') }} Admission

Free for Everyone

Learners, staff, alumni, and guests are welcome at no cost. Group visits and guided tours can be arranged in advance.

Getting Here

Enter through the main gate of General Tinio National High School and follow the museum signs toward Heritage Hall. Visitors arriving by private vehicle may use the designated drop-off area near the administration building.

For guided group visits, please coordinate with the Heritage Committee ahead of your arrival so a docent can meet your group at the entrance.

On Campus

Famous Landmarks of GTNHS

These are the landmarks that anchor daily life at General Tinio National High School and hold a place in the school's history.

{{ icons.icon('landmark', 'w-5 h-5') }} Landmark

Heritage Hall

Home of the school museum and digital archive, located on the ground floor beside the main administration building.

{{ icons.icon('history', 'w-5 h-5') }} Landmark

Sotto Hall

One of the school's two spacious halls. Fully rehabilitated and air-conditioned, it is now used for programs and assemblies.

{{ icons.icon('award', 'w-5 h-5') }} Landmark

"Dream Come True" Gymnasium

The multi-purpose gymnasium donated by the Provincial Government, which materialized in 2013. It hosts sports and school-wide events.

{{ icons.icon('grid', 'w-5 h-5') }} Landmark

The School Quadrangle

Built under Dr. Cecilio M. Tulaban, it is a venue for school activities and the Papaya Festival Centennial Celebration Street Dance Competition.

{{ icons.icon('users', 'w-5 h-5') }} Landmark

PPP Building

A two-storey building with 18 classrooms, donated through the Public-Private Partnership program.

{{ icons.icon('book-open', 'w-5 h-5') }} Landmark

JICA Building

An eight-room building donated by the Japanese government, added alongside the Villareal Building.

{{ icons.icon('sparkles', 'w-5 h-5') }} Landmark

Villareal Building

A classroom building that helped raise the school's classrooms from thirty-five to forty-five.

{{ icons.icon('pin', 'w-5 h-5') }} Landmark

Multi-Purpose Hall

The second of the school's two spacious halls, used for gatherings, activities, and school events.
